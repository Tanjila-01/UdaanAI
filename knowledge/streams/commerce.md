# Commerce Stream

## Description
The Commerce stream focuses on business, accounting, economics and finance, preparing students for careers in accountancy, business management, banking and finance.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> PUC (11-12) Commerce (with or without Mathematics) -> B.Com, BBA, CA, CS, CMA, or related business/finance degrees

## Important subjects
Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Mathematics/Statistics (optional but recommended for finance-heavy careers)

## Important skills
Numerical ability, analytical thinking, understanding of business concepts, attention to detail, communication

## Suitable interests
Business, finance and numbers, economics, entrepreneurship, structured career qualifications like CA/CS

## Alternative pathways
Commerce without Mathematics is suitable for students planning B.Com, BBA or law; Commerce with Mathematics is recommended for CA, actuarial science, or economics-heavy programs

## Karnataka context
Bengaluru's status as a business and financial hub makes Commerce a strong stream for students targeting finance, accounting and management careers in the state.
