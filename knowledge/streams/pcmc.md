# Science Stream — PCMC (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science)

## Description
PCMC is a Science stream combination focused on Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Computer Science, chosen by students aiming for engineering, computer science and technology-related careers.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> PUC (11-12) PCMC -> B.E./B.Tech, BCA, B.Sc Computer Science, or related technology degrees

## Important subjects
Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science

## Important skills
Logical reasoning, problem solving, quantitative aptitude, programming fundamentals (often introduced at PUC level)

## Suitable interests
Technology, mathematics, computers, engineering, building and problem solving

## Alternative pathways
Students can also move to BCA or B.Sc Computer Science instead of core engineering if they prefer a shorter or less math-intensive route into technology careers

## Karnataka context
PCMC is offered by Karnataka's Department of Pre-University Education and is a common preparatory combination for KCET and COMEDK aspirants targeting computer science and engineering seats.
