# Arts / Humanities Stream

## Description
The Arts or Humanities stream covers subjects such as History, Political Science, Economics, Psychology, Sociology and Languages, preparing students for careers in law, civil services, media, design, social work and the humanities.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> PUC (11-12) Arts/Humanities -> B.A. (various specializations), B.A. LL.B, journalism/mass communication degrees, or design programs

## Important subjects
History, Political Science, Economics, Psychology, Sociology, Languages

## Important skills
Reading and writing, critical thinking, research, communication, empathy and social awareness

## Suitable interests
Social issues, literature and languages, psychology, civic and political affairs, creative fields

## Alternative pathways
Students interested in design can move into B.Des programs; those interested in law can pursue integrated B.A. LL.B; those interested in government service can prepare for civil services exams after any bachelor's degree

## Karnataka context
Karnataka's PUC boards offer a range of Arts/Humanities combinations, and Bengaluru hosts several reputed colleges for Arts, journalism, design and law.
