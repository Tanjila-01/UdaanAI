# Science Stream — PCMB (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology)

## Description
PCMB is a Science stream combination that includes both Mathematics and Biology, keeping open both engineering and medical career pathways at the PUC stage before final specialization.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> PUC (11-12) PCMB -> MBBS, BDS, B.Sc Nursing, B.E./B.Tech, or other Science-based degrees

## Important subjects
Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology

## Important skills
Scientific reasoning, memorization and conceptual understanding, quantitative aptitude, biological/anatomical understanding

## Suitable interests
Science broadly, keeping medical and engineering options open simultaneously, biology and health alongside mathematics

## Alternative pathways
Students who decide firmly on medicine can take PCMB and skip Mathematics-heavy electives later; students who decide on engineering can proceed via Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics results alone

## Karnataka context
PCMB is a common choice among Karnataka PUC students who are undecided between NEET (medical) and KCET/JEE (engineering) at the time of choosing a stream.
