# KCET (Karnataka Common Entrance Test)

## Description
KCET is Karnataka's state-level entrance examination for admission to undergraduate Engineering, Architecture, Pharmacy and certain agriculture-related degree programs at colleges within the state.

## Eligibility
Completion of 2nd PUC (Class 12) with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM) for engineering; PCB or relevant subjects for other eligible programs; Karnataka domicile requirements apply for certain quota seats.

## Exam structure
Typically covers Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology, conducted by the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA).

## What it leads to
Admission to Karnataka's engineering colleges (including government and many private colleges), architecture programs and select other professional courses through centralized counseling.

## Alternative or parallel exams
JEE Main (for NITs and some engineering colleges), COMEDK UGET (for a separate set of private engineering colleges in Karnataka)

## Karnataka context
KCET is the primary route through which most Karnataka-domiciled students secure engineering seats within the state, alongside reserved quotas for various categories.
