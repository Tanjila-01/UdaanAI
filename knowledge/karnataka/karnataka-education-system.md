# Karnataka Education System — Structure Overview

## Description
Karnataka follows a structure of Class 1-10 (SSLC board exam at the end of Class 10), followed by PUC (Class 11-12, Karnataka's version of "Plus Two"), and then undergraduate degree programs.

## Key stages
1. Primary and Secondary school through Class 10, ending in the SSLC board examination
2. PUC (Pre-University Course), 2 years, with a stream choice of Science, Commerce or Arts
3. Undergraduate degree (B.E./B.Tech, B.Sc, B.Com, B.A., BCA, BBA, MBBS, etc.) or vocational routes (Diploma, ITI)
4. Postgraduate or professional study where applicable (M.Tech, MBA, M.Sc, CA, etc.)

## Key state bodies and exams
Karnataka Department of Pre-University Education (PUC), Karnataka Secondary Education Examination Board (SSLC), Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA, conducts KCET), Directorate of Technical Education (diploma admissions), Directorate of Employment and Training (ITI admissions)

## Alternative early-exit pathways
After Class 10, students can choose PUC (academic route), Diploma in Engineering (3-year technical route), or ITI (short-term skill-based route), each leading to different career timelines.

## Karnataka context
Understanding this structure helps students and families choose the right pathway after Class 10 based on their interests, whether they favor an academic route via PUC or an earlier hands-on technical route via Diploma or ITI.
