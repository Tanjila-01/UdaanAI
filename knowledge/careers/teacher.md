# Teacher / Educator

## Description
A teacher educates students in schools or colleges, planning lessons, delivering instruction and assessing student progress across a chosen subject area and age group.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> any stream (Science, Commerce or Arts, based on subject specialization desired) -> PUC (11-12) -> Bachelor's degree in the subject -> B.Ed (Bachelor of Education)

## Important subjects
Subject-specific mastery (Mathematics, Science, Languages, Social Science, etc.), Psychology helpful for understanding learners

## Important skills
Communication, patience, subject expertise, classroom management, empathy, adaptability

## Suitable interests
Teaching and mentoring, a specific academic subject, working with children or young adults

## Alternative pathways
Integrated B.A./B.Sc B.Ed programs; Montessori/early-childhood education certifications for pre-primary teaching; M.Ed for higher-education or administrative roles

## Typical entry routes and exams
Karnataka TET (Teacher Eligibility Test) is required for government school teaching positions after B.Ed

## Career progression
Assistant Teacher -> Senior Teacher / Subject Head -> Vice Principal -> Principal, or a track into curriculum design, educational content development, or school administration.

## Industry sectors and typical employers
Government schools, aided and private schools, international/CBSE/ICSE schools, coaching institutes, and ed-tech companies for content or online-teaching roles.

## Related job titles
Subject Teacher, Curriculum Developer, Academic Coordinator, Private Tutor, Ed-tech Content Creator

## Recommended certifications and further learning
M.Ed for those aiming at higher-education teaching or school leadership roles; subject-specific advanced degrees strengthen candidacy for senior or specialized teaching positions.

## Challenges and considerations
Government teaching positions can be competitive and require clearing Karnataka TET plus recruitment exams; private-school pay can vary widely by institution; the role offers strong job stability and social impact, particularly in government positions after selection.

## Karnataka context
Karnataka has a large network of government, aided and private schools, with Karnataka TET as the state-level eligibility requirement for government teaching posts.
