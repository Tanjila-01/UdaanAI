# Doctor (MBBS)

## Description
A doctor diagnoses and treats illness, promotes health and provides medical care to patients, working in hospitals, clinics or private practice, with the option to specialize after MBBS.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Science / PCMB -> PUC (11-12) with Biology -> MBBS (5.5 years including internship) -> optional MD/MS specialization

## Important subjects
Biology, Chemistry, Physics

## Important skills
Scientific reasoning, patience, communication with patients, decision-making under pressure, attention to detail, empathy

## Suitable interests
Biology and human health, helping people, science, long-term academic commitment

## Alternative pathways
BDS (Dentistry), BAMS (Ayurveda), BHMS (Homeopathy), B.Sc Nursing, or allied health science degrees for students interested in the medical field with different entry requirements

## Typical entry routes and exams
NEET-UG is mandatory for admission to MBBS/BDS across India, including Karnataka's government and private medical colleges

## Career progression
MBBS graduate -> compulsory rotating internship -> general practice, or NEET-PG for MD/MS specialization (e.g., cardiology, surgery, pediatrics) -> Consultant / Specialist, with further super-specialization (DM/MCh) possible after that.

## Industry sectors and typical employers
Government hospitals, private hospital chains, primary health centers, research institutions, and independent private practice after sufficient experience.

## Related job titles
General Physician, Medical Officer, Resident Doctor, and, after specialization, roles such as Surgeon, Pediatrician, Cardiologist or Radiologist

## Recommended certifications and further learning
NEET-PG for postgraduate specialization, continuing medical education (CME) credits required to maintain registration, specialty-specific fellowships for advanced practice.

## Challenges and considerations
The full pathway to becoming a specialist is long (MBBS + internship + PG + possible super-specialization can span 10+ years); the work is demanding, often involves long or irregular hours, and requires sustained academic commitment; it is also one of the most respected and stable career paths in terms of long-term demand.

## Karnataka context
Karnataka has a large number of government and private medical colleges, with admission through NEET-UG and state/private counseling processes.
