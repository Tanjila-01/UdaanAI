# AI / Machine Learning Engineer

## Description
An AI/ML engineer builds systems that learn from data, including recommendation engines, computer vision, natural language processing and generative AI applications. The role blends software engineering with applied mathematics and statistics.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Science / PCMC -> PUC (11-12) -> B.E./B.Tech Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, or Information Science -> optional M.Tech/M.S. specialization

## Important subjects
Mathematics, Computer Science, Physics

## Important skills
Programming (Python), linear algebra and calculus, machine learning frameworks, data structures and algorithms, problem solving, continuous learning

## Suitable interests
Technology, mathematics, research, building intelligent systems, experimentation

## Alternative pathways
B.Sc Computer Science or Mathematics followed by specialized AI/ML postgraduate programs or certifications; transition from a general software engineering role into ML teams

## Typical entry routes and exams
KCET, JEE Main, COMEDK for engineering colleges offering dedicated AI/ML branches

## Career progression
Junior ML Engineer -> ML Engineer -> Senior ML Engineer -> ML Lead / AI Architect, or a research-oriented track toward Applied Scientist/Research Scientist roles, often supported by a master's degree.

## Industry sectors and typical employers
AI-focused product companies, research labs, large technology companies' AI divisions, healthtech, fintech, and startups building generative AI or computer-vision products.

## Related job titles
Machine Learning Engineer, Applied Scientist, NLP Engineer, Computer Vision Engineer, Research Engineer

## Recommended certifications and further learning
Deep learning specializations (e.g., coursework covering neural networks and transformers), contributing to open-source ML projects, participating in ML competitions, building and deploying end-to-end model projects.

## Challenges and considerations
A fast-moving field requiring continuous learning; strong mathematics (linear algebra, probability, calculus) is genuinely necessary, not optional; entry-level roles are competitive and often favor candidates with demonstrable projects or research experience.

## Karnataka context
Karnataka has a growing number of engineering colleges offering dedicated Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning branches, alongside strong industry demand in Bengaluru.
