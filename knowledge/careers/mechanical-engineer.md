# Mechanical Engineer

## Description
A mechanical engineer designs, develops, manufactures and maintains mechanical systems such as machines, engines, tools and manufacturing equipment. Work spans automotive, aerospace, manufacturing, energy and robotics sectors.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Science / PCMB or PCMC -> PUC (11-12) -> B.E./B.Tech Mechanical Engineering

## Important subjects
Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry

## Important skills
Technical drawing, CAD software, problem solving, understanding of physics and materials, teamwork, manufacturing processes

## Suitable interests
Machines, how things work, design, physics, hands-on building

## Alternative pathways
Diploma in Mechanical Engineering after Class 10, followed by lateral entry into the second year of B.E.

## Typical entry routes and exams
KCET, JEE Main, COMEDK for engineering colleges

## Career progression
Graduate Engineer Trainee -> Design/Production Engineer -> Senior Engineer -> Engineering Manager / Project Manager, or specialization into robotics, automotive design, or manufacturing plant management.

## Industry sectors and typical employers
Automotive manufacturers, aerospace companies, heavy machinery and industrial equipment firms, manufacturing plants, energy companies and engineering consultancies.

## Related job titles
Design Engineer, Production Engineer, Quality Engineer, Maintenance Engineer, R&D Engineer

## Recommended certifications and further learning
CAD/CAM software proficiency (AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CATIA), Six Sigma/quality-management certifications, internships in manufacturing plants for hands-on exposure.

## Challenges and considerations
Some roles require working on factory floors or industrial sites rather than purely in an office; growth into design/R&D roles often benefits from a postgraduate specialization; automation is changing traditional manufacturing roles, making continuous upskilling valuable.

## Karnataka context
Karnataka has a strong automotive, aerospace and manufacturing base, particularly around Bengaluru and Belagavi, offering opportunities for mechanical engineers.
