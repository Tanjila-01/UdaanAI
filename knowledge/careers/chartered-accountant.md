# Chartered Accountant (CA)

## Description
A chartered accountant manages financial records, audits accounts, ensures tax compliance and advises businesses on financial strategy. CAs work in accounting firms, corporations or independent practice.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Commerce (with or without Mathematics) -> PUC (11-12) -> CA Foundation -> CA Intermediate -> Articleship -> CA Final (via ICAI)

## Important subjects
Accountancy, Mathematics, Economics, Business Studies

## Important skills
Numerical accuracy, analytical thinking, attention to detail, ethics and integrity, communication, time management

## Suitable interests
Numbers and finance, business, structured problem solving, independent study

## Alternative pathways
B.Com followed by CA, or pursuing Company Secretary (CS) / Cost and Management Accountant (CMA) as related finance qualifications

## Typical entry routes and exams
CA Foundation, Intermediate and Final examinations are conducted by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI); no separate state entrance exam is required

## Career progression
Articled Assistant (during articleship) -> Qualified CA -> Senior Associate/Manager at a firm, or Finance Manager/Controller within a company, progressing toward Partner (in practice) or CFO (in industry) with experience.

## Industry sectors and typical employers
Chartered accountancy firms (including the "Big Four" and mid-sized firms), corporate finance and accounting departments, banks and financial institutions, and independent practice.

## Related job titles
Auditor, Tax Consultant, Financial Analyst, Internal Auditor, Finance Manager

## Recommended certifications and further learning
CS (Company Secretary) or CMA (Cost and Management Accountant) as complementary qualifications, CFA for those moving toward investment/finance analysis roles, continuing professional education required by ICAI.

## Challenges and considerations
The CA path is rigorous, with a demanding syllabus and a mandatory 2-3 year articleship (practical training) alongside study; pass rates at each level are relatively low, requiring sustained discipline; the qualification is highly respected and portable across industries once completed.

## Karnataka context
Bengaluru's status as a major business and financial hub provides strong opportunities for CAs in both firms and corporate finance roles.
