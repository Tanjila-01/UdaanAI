# Graphic / UI-UX Designer

## Description
A graphic or UI/UX designer creates visual content, branding, layouts and digital interfaces for websites, apps and marketing materials, combining creativity with an understanding of user needs.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> any stream (Arts/Humanities is common, though not mandatory) -> PUC (11-12) -> Bachelor's degree or diploma in Design (B.Des, Fine Arts, or UI/UX-focused programs)

## Important subjects
Art, Computer Science (for digital design tools), English/Communication

## Important skills
Visual design software (Adobe Creative Suite, Figma), creativity, typography and color theory, user-experience thinking, communication with clients/teams

## Suitable interests
Art and visual creativity, design, storytelling through images, technology

## Alternative pathways
Self-taught portfolio-based entry into design roles, short-term diploma courses in graphic design or UI/UX, B.A. Fine Arts

## Typical entry routes and exams
NID DAT, UCEED, or institute-specific entrance tests for premier design programs; many design schools also admit via portfolio review

## Career progression
Junior Designer -> Designer -> Senior Designer -> Design Lead / Creative Director, or a specialization track into Product Design, Brand Design, or Design Research/Strategy.

## Industry sectors and typical employers
Design studios and agencies, in-house design teams at technology and product companies, advertising and branding agencies, freelance/independent practice, and publishing/media houses.

## Related job titles
UI Designer, UX Designer, Product Designer, Visual Designer, Brand Designer

## Recommended certifications and further learning
Building a strong portfolio is generally more important than certifications; UX-research and design-thinking courses, and tool proficiency (Figma, Adobe XD) help; internships at design studios or startups provide valuable real-world experience.

## Challenges and considerations
It is a portfolio-driven field where demonstrated work often matters more than credentials; freelance and early-career income can be variable; strong communication skills are needed to justify design decisions to clients or product teams.

## Karnataka context
Bengaluru's active startup and product-design ecosystem creates strong demand for UI/UX and product designers.
