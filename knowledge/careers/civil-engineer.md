# Civil Engineer

## Description
A civil engineer plans, designs, constructs and maintains infrastructure such as buildings, roads, bridges, dams and water systems. Work includes structural design, site supervision and project management.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Science / PCMB or PCMC -> PUC (11-12) -> B.E./B.Tech Civil Engineering

## Important subjects
Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry

## Important skills
Technical drawing, structural analysis, project management, CAD/design software, attention to detail, on-site problem solving

## Suitable interests
Construction, infrastructure, design, physics, working outdoors and on-site

## Alternative pathways
Diploma in Civil Engineering after Class 10, followed by lateral entry into the second year of B.E.

## Typical entry routes and exams
KCET, JEE Main, COMEDK for engineering colleges

## Career progression
Graduate Engineer Trainee -> Site/Design Engineer -> Project Engineer -> Project Manager / Chief Engineer, or specialization into structural design, urban planning, or infrastructure consulting.

## Industry sectors and typical employers
Construction companies, real estate developers, government infrastructure departments (PWD, municipal bodies), engineering consultancies, and large infrastructure/EPC (engineering-procurement-construction) firms.

## Related job titles
Structural Engineer, Site Engineer, Project Engineer, Urban Planner, Construction Manager

## Recommended certifications and further learning
CAD and structural-design software (AutoCAD, STAAD.Pro, Revit), project-management certifications, hands-on site internships during the degree.

## Challenges and considerations
Work often involves significant on-site time, sometimes in outdoor or remote locations; project timelines can be demanding; growth into senior design or planning roles is aided by a postgraduate specialization or professional licensing.

## Karnataka context
Rapid urban development in Bengaluru and other Karnataka cities creates steady demand for civil engineers in construction and infrastructure projects.
