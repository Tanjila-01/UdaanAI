# Data Scientist

## Description
A data scientist analyzes large datasets to find patterns, builds statistical and machine-learning models, and helps organizations make data-driven decisions. Work involves data cleaning, analysis, visualization and model building.

## Day-to-day work
Exploring and cleaning raw data, running statistical analyses, building and testing predictive models, creating dashboards and visualizations, presenting findings to non-technical stakeholders, and collaborating with engineers to deploy models into products.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Science / PCMC -> PUC (11-12) -> B.E./B.Tech Computer Science, B.Sc Statistics/Mathematics, or B.Sc Data Science -> optional M.Sc/PG diploma in Data Science

## Important subjects
Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science

## Important skills
Statistics and probability, Python or R programming, data visualization, SQL, machine learning fundamentals, communication of insights

## Suitable interests
Mathematics, patterns and numbers, technology, research, problem solving

## Alternative pathways
B.Sc Statistics/Mathematics/Economics followed by a PG diploma or master's in Data Science/Analytics; online certifications combined with a core STEM or commerce degree

## Typical entry routes and exams
KCET, JEE Main, COMEDK for engineering entry; direct admission or entrance tests for B.Sc Statistics/Data Science programs

## Career progression
Data Analyst / Junior Data Scientist -> Data Scientist -> Senior Data Scientist -> Lead Data Scientist / Analytics Manager, or a specialization track into Machine Learning Engineering or Data Science leadership roles.

## Industry sectors and typical employers
Technology and product companies, e-commerce, fintech and banking analytics teams, healthcare analytics, consulting firms and dedicated analytics/AI companies.

## Related job titles
Data Analyst, Machine Learning Engineer, Business Intelligence Analyst, Research Analyst, Quantitative Analyst

## Recommended certifications and further learning
Applied statistics and machine learning courses, Kaggle competitions for hands-on practice, SQL and cloud data-platform certifications, a portfolio of analysis projects.

## Challenges and considerations
Requires comfort with both mathematics and programming; entry-level roles often expect a portfolio of independent projects since formal data-science-specific degrees are still relatively new in India; communicating technical findings clearly to non-technical audiences is a key differentiator.

## Karnataka context
Bengaluru's concentration of technology and analytics companies creates strong demand for data science talent in the state.
