# Lawyer

## Description
A lawyer advises clients on legal matters, represents them in court or negotiations, and drafts legal documents. Lawyers may specialize in corporate law, criminal law, civil law or other areas.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> any stream (Arts/Humanities, Commerce or Science) -> PUC (11-12) -> integrated 5-year B.A. LL.B / B.B.A. LL.B, or 3-year LL.B after any bachelor's degree

## Important subjects
English, Social Science/Political Science, Economics

## Important skills
Analytical reasoning, reading and writing, argumentation, research, public speaking, attention to detail

## Suitable interests
Debate and argumentation, social and civic issues, reading, structured reasoning

## Alternative pathways
Complete a bachelor's degree in any stream and pursue a 3-year LL.B afterward instead of the 5-year integrated route

## Typical entry routes and exams
CLAT (Common Law Admission Test) for National Law Universities; Karnataka also has state-level law entrance processes for other law colleges

## Career progression
Junior Associate at a law firm or under a senior advocate -> Associate -> Senior Associate -> Partner (in a firm), or independent practice building toward a personal client base; some lawyers move into corporate in-house legal roles or the judiciary via further exams.

## Industry sectors and typical employers
Law firms (litigation or corporate), corporate legal departments, government legal services, the judiciary (via judicial service exams), and NGOs/public-interest law organizations.

## Related job titles
Corporate Lawyer, Litigation Associate, Legal Advisor, Legal Analyst, Public Prosecutor

## Recommended certifications and further learning
An LL.M for specialization (corporate law, intellectual property, etc.) or academic/judicial careers; moot court participation and internships during the degree are highly valued by recruiters.

## Challenges and considerations
Early-career litigation work can involve long hours and modest initial income while building a practice; corporate law roles at firms tend to offer more structured career progression; strong reading, writing and argumentation skills are essential and worth developing early.

## Karnataka context
Karnataka is home to the National Law School of India University (NLSIU) in Bengaluru, one of India's premier law schools.
