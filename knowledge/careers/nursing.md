# Nurse

## Description
A nurse provides patient care, administers treatments, monitors patient health and supports doctors in hospitals, clinics and community health settings.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Science / PCMB -> PUC (11-12) with Biology -> B.Sc Nursing (4 years) or GNM Diploma (3 years)

## Important subjects
Biology, Chemistry, Physics

## Important skills
Patient care, attention to detail, communication, empathy, ability to work under pressure, teamwork with medical staff

## Suitable interests
Helping people, biology and health, hands-on caregiving

## Alternative pathways
GNM (General Nursing and Midwifery) diploma as a shorter alternative to B.Sc Nursing, with the option to upgrade via Post Basic B.Sc Nursing later

## Typical entry routes and exams
Admission is generally through institute-level entrance tests or merit in PUC; some states use a common entrance test for nursing programs

## Career progression
Staff Nurse -> Senior Staff Nurse -> Nursing Supervisor / Ward In-charge -> Nursing Superintendent, or specialization into ICU/critical care, pediatric, or operation-theatre nursing; further study (M.Sc Nursing) opens teaching and administrative roles.

## Industry sectors and typical employers
Government and private hospitals, nursing homes, community health centers, home healthcare services, and increasingly, opportunities abroad in countries with nursing shortages.

## Related job titles
Staff Nurse, ICU Nurse, Community Health Nurse, Nurse Educator, Nurse Practitioner (with further qualification)

## Recommended certifications and further learning
Post Basic B.Sc Nursing for GNM diploma holders wanting a degree upgrade, specialized critical-care or midwifery certifications, and language/international certification (e.g., for Gulf or Western job markets) for those considering overseas opportunities.

## Challenges and considerations
The work can involve physically and emotionally demanding shifts, including night duty; it offers strong job security and meaningful patient impact; nursing is also one of the more accessible healthcare careers with good international mobility.

## Karnataka context
Karnataka's large hospital network, including major private and government hospitals in Bengaluru, offers strong employment opportunities for nurses.
