# Cybersecurity Engineer

## Description
A cybersecurity engineer protects computer systems, networks and data from unauthorized access, attacks and breaches. Work includes security monitoring, vulnerability assessment, incident response and building secure systems.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Science / PCMC -> PUC (11-12) -> B.E./B.Tech Computer Science, Information Science, or Cybersecurity -> industry certifications (CEH, CompTIA Security+, CISSP)

## Important subjects
Mathematics, Computer Science, Physics

## Important skills
Networking fundamentals, operating systems, programming/scripting, ethical hacking concepts, analytical thinking, attention to detail

## Suitable interests
Technology, problem solving, investigation, systems and security

## Alternative pathways
BCA or B.Sc Computer Science followed by cybersecurity certifications; diploma in computer science with lateral entry to a security-focused B.E.

## Typical entry routes and exams
KCET, JEE Main, COMEDK for engineering entry; professional certifications matter significantly for this field alongside the degree

## Career progression
Security Analyst -> Security Engineer -> Senior Security Engineer -> Security Architect / CISO (Chief Information Security Officer) track, or specialization into penetration testing, threat intelligence or incident response leadership.

## Industry sectors and typical employers
IT services and consulting firms, banks and financial institutions, government and defense-related agencies, dedicated cybersecurity companies, and in-house security teams at large technology companies.

## Related job titles
Security Analyst, Penetration Tester / Ethical Hacker, Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst, Information Security Consultant, Incident Response Analyst

## Recommended certifications and further learning
CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker), CompTIA Security+, CISSP (for experienced professionals), hands-on practice through capture-the-flag (CTF) competitions and home-lab experiments.

## Challenges and considerations
Requires staying current with constantly evolving threats and attack techniques; some roles involve on-call or shift-based monitoring work; a strong ethical grounding is essential since the skills involved can be misused if applied irresponsibly.

## Karnataka context
Bengaluru's large IT and banking sector presence has driven strong demand for cybersecurity professionals across the state.
