# B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce)

## Description
B.Com is a 3-year undergraduate degree focused on accounting, finance, taxation and business law, commonly pursued by Commerce-stream students and a common precursor to CA, CS or an MBA.

## Eligibility
Completion of PUC (Class 12) in Commerce, though some colleges also admit students from other streams.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Commerce -> PUC (11-12) -> B.Com -> optional CA/CS/CMA or M.Com/MBA

## Important subjects at PUC level
Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics

## Entrance exams
Most colleges admit based on PUC merit; some autonomous colleges conduct their own entrance or aptitude tests

## Alternative pathways
BBA for a more management-oriented alternative; direct entry into CA Foundation alongside or instead of B.Com for students focused specifically on chartered accountancy

## Karnataka context
Bengaluru's finance and corporate sector offers strong opportunities for B.Com graduates in accounting, auditing and finance support roles.
