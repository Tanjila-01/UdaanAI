# B.E. / B.Tech (Engineering Degree)

## Description
B.E. (Bachelor of Engineering) and B.Tech (Bachelor of Technology) are 4-year undergraduate engineering degrees offered across branches such as Computer Science, Mechanical, Civil, Electronics and Electrical Engineering.

## Eligibility
Completion of PUC (Class 12) with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM) as compulsory subjects.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Science / PCMB or PCMC -> PUC (11-12) with PCM -> B.E./B.Tech

## Important subjects at PUC level
Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics

## Entrance exams
KCET (Karnataka Common Entrance Test), JEE Main, COMEDK UGET for admission to engineering colleges in Karnataka

## Common specializations
Computer Science, Information Science, Electronics and Communication, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

## Alternative pathways
Diploma in Engineering after Class 10, followed by lateral entry directly into the second year of B.E./B.Tech, skipping the first year

## Karnataka context
Karnataka has one of the largest networks of engineering colleges in India, with KCET as the primary state-level entrance exam for admission.
