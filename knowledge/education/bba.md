# BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)

## Description
BBA is a 3-year undergraduate degree focused on business management, covering areas such as marketing, finance, human resources and operations, often followed by an MBA.

## Eligibility
Completion of PUC (Class 12) in any stream, though Commerce is the most common background.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Commerce (or any stream) -> PUC (11-12) -> BBA -> optional MBA

## Important subjects at PUC level
Business Studies, Economics, Mathematics/Statistics (helpful but not always mandatory)

## Entrance exams
Many colleges admit via PUC merit; some private universities and premier institutes conduct entrance tests

## Alternative pathways
B.Com as a more accounting-focused alternative; direct entry into family or small businesses combined with part-time management education

## Karnataka context
Bengaluru's business and startup ecosystem provides strong internship and placement opportunities for BBA graduates.
