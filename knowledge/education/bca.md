# BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)

## Description
BCA is a 3-year undergraduate degree focused on computer applications, programming and software development, offering a shorter alternative to B.E./B.Tech for entering the technology field.

## Eligibility
Completion of PUC (Class 12) in any stream, though a Science or Commerce background with Mathematics is preferred by many colleges.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> any stream (Science preferred) -> PUC (11-12) -> BCA -> optional MCA for further specialization

## Important subjects at PUC level
Mathematics or Computer Science (where required by the college), English

## Entrance exams
Most colleges admit based on PUC merit; some private universities conduct their own entrance tests

## Alternative pathways
B.Sc Computer Science as a similar alternative; MCA after BCA for students wanting a master's-level qualification equivalent to B.E./B.Tech graduates

## Karnataka context
Bengaluru's IT industry widely recruits BCA graduates for software development and support roles, making it a practical shorter route into technology careers.
