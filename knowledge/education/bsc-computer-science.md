# B.Sc Computer Science

## Description
B.Sc Computer Science is a 3-year undergraduate degree emphasizing the theoretical and practical foundations of computer science, including programming, algorithms and computer systems.

## Eligibility
Completion of PUC (Class 12) in Science, generally with Mathematics or Computer Science as a subject.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Science / PCMC -> PUC (11-12) -> B.Sc Computer Science -> optional M.Sc Computer Science

## Important subjects at PUC level
Mathematics, Computer Science, Physics

## Entrance exams
Most colleges admit based on PUC merit; some autonomous colleges and universities conduct entrance tests

## Alternative pathways
BCA as a closely related alternative with a more applications-oriented curriculum; B.E./B.Tech Computer Science for a more engineering-focused route

## Karnataka context
Several Karnataka universities and autonomous colleges offer well-regarded B.Sc Computer Science programs feeding into the state's technology industry.
