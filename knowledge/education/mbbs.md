# MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery)

## Description
MBBS is a 5.5-year undergraduate medical degree (including a mandatory 1-year internship) that qualifies graduates to practice as doctors.

## Eligibility
Completion of PUC (Class 12) with Physics, Chemistry and Biology (PCB), plus a qualifying score in NEET-UG.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Science / PCMB -> PUC (11-12) with PCB -> NEET-UG -> MBBS -> optional MD/MS specialization

## Important subjects at PUC level
Biology, Chemistry, Physics

## Entrance exams
NEET-UG is compulsory nationwide for MBBS/BDS admission, including all government and private medical colleges in Karnataka

## Alternative pathways
BDS (Dentistry), BAMS (Ayurveda), BHMS (Homeopathy), B.Sc Nursing or Allied Health Sciences for students who do not qualify for MBBS but wish to remain in the medical field

## Karnataka context
Karnataka has a large number of government and private medical colleges, with seats allocated through NEET-based state counseling in addition to central counseling.
