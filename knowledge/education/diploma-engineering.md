# Diploma in Engineering

## Description
A Diploma in Engineering is a 3-year technical program that can be pursued directly after Class 10, offering an earlier and more hands-on entry into engineering fields compared to PUC followed by B.E./B.Tech.

## Eligibility
Completion of Class 10 (SSLC); no PUC required before joining.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> Diploma in Engineering (3 years) -> direct employment, or lateral entry into the second year of B.E./B.Tech

## Important subjects at Class 10 level
Mathematics, Science

## Entrance exams
Admission in Karnataka is generally through DTE (Department of Technical Education) counseling based on Class 10 marks; lateral entry to B.E. uses a separate diploma-to-degree entrance process

## Common specializations
Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Electronics, Computer Science Engineering (diploma level)

## Alternative pathways
PUC followed by direct B.E./B.Tech admission for students who prefer the standard 4-year engineering route instead of the diploma-plus-lateral-entry path

## Karnataka context
Karnataka's polytechnic colleges offer diploma engineering programs as a widely used pathway for students who want an earlier, cost-effective entry into technical careers.
