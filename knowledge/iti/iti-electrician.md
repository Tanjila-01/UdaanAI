# ITI Trade — Electrician

## Description
The Electrician trade at ITI trains students in electrical wiring, installation, maintenance and repair of electrical systems and equipment, leading to skilled trade employment.

## Eligibility
Completion of Class 10 (SSLC); course duration is typically 2 years.

## Recommended path
Class 10 -> ITI Electrician trade (2 years) -> apprenticeship (often under the Apprentices Act) -> employment as an electrician or electrical technician

## Important subjects at Class 10 level
Mathematics, Science

## Important skills
Electrical wiring, circuit understanding, safety practices, tool handling, troubleshooting

## Suitable interests
Hands-on electrical work, practical problem solving, wanting quicker entry into skilled employment

## Alternative pathways
Diploma in Electrical Engineering for a broader and longer technical qualification; further ITI-to-diploma lateral entry options exist for motivated students

## Karnataka context
Electricians trained through Karnataka's ITIs find employment across residential, industrial and infrastructure projects statewide, including in Bengaluru's construction sector.
