# ITI (Industrial Training Institute) — Overview

## Description
ITI courses are short-term (6 months to 2 years) vocational training programs that prepare students for skilled trade jobs such as electrician, fitter, welder, plumber and mechanic. ITIs offer one of the fastest routes to employment after Class 10.

## Eligibility
Completion of Class 10 (SSLC) or Class 8 depending on the trade; specific eligibility varies by trade and institute.

## Recommended school path
Class 10 -> ITI trade course (6 months to 2 years) -> apprenticeship or direct employment; optional further study via diploma lateral entry for some trades

## Important subjects at Class 10 level
Mathematics, Science (basic proficiency helpful for technical trades)

## Entrance exams
Admission in Karnataka is generally through the Directorate of Employment and Training based on Class 10 marks; some trades may have a basic aptitude assessment

## Suitable interests
Hands-on technical work, mechanical or electrical systems, wanting to start earning sooner rather than pursuing a longer degree

## Alternative pathways
Diploma in Engineering as a comparable but longer and broader technical alternative; ITI graduates in some trades can pursue lateral entry into diploma programs

## Karnataka context
Karnataka has a wide network of government and private ITIs, and ITI-trained workers are in demand across the state's manufacturing, construction and automotive sectors.
