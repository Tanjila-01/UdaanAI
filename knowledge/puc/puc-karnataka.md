# PUC (Pre-University Course) in Karnataka

## Description
PUC refers to Class 11 and 12 in Karnataka, administered by the Karnataka Department of Pre-University Education. It is the stage at which students choose a stream (Science, Commerce or Arts) that shapes their subsequent degree and career options.

## Structure
Two years (1st PUC and 2nd PUC), typically taken after Class 10 (SSLC), concluding with the 2nd PUC board examination.

## Streams offered
Science (with combinations such as PCMB, PCMC, PCMB+Computer Science), Commerce (with or without Mathematics), Arts/Humanities

## Why stream choice matters
The subjects taken at PUC determine eligibility for entrance exams and degree programs afterward: PCMB is required for NEET/medical programs, PCM is required for most engineering entrance exams, and Commerce/Arts subjects shape eligibility for business, law and humanities degrees.

## Important exams during/after PUC
2nd PUC board examination (Karnataka PUE Board), followed by entrance exams relevant to the chosen stream such as NEET-UG, KCET, JEE Main, COMEDK or CLAT

## Alternative pathways
Students who prefer an earlier vocational or technical route can choose a Diploma in Engineering or an ITI trade after Class 10 instead of PUC

## Karnataka context
PUC is specific to Karnataka's education structure (equivalent to Class 11-12 or "Plus Two" in other states) and is a prerequisite for most undergraduate admissions in the state.
