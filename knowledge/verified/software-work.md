# Software development: work overview

## What software developers do
Software developers understand what users need, design applications or systems, and plan how their components fit together. Their work includes maintaining software and documenting it for future changes. Software quality assurance and testing roles focus on finding defects and reporting problems.

## Collaboration and scope
Development often involves working with other developers and testers. This overview describes occupational duties from a United States government source. It does not establish Indian college eligibility, required school streams, salaries or job guarantees.
