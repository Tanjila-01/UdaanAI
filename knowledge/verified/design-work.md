# Graphic design: work overview

## What graphic designers do
Graphic designers use images, typography and layouts to communicate ideas. They may develop logos, illustrations and materials such as advertisements or brochures. Work includes understanding a client's brief, presenting ideas, incorporating feedback and checking a design before publication.

## Scope
This overview covers graphic design duties described by a United States government source. It does not establish UI/UX qualifications, Indian design-college admission requirements, salaries or employment guarantees.
