# Electrician: work overview

## What electricians do
Electricians install and maintain electrical wiring, lighting and control systems. They read technical diagrams, inspect components, diagnose faults with testing equipment, and repair or replace equipment. Work may involve collaborating with other construction professionals.

## Scope
This overview describes occupational duties from a United States government source. It does not establish Karnataka ITI admissions, apprenticeship eligibility or local licensing rules, and is not an electrical repair tutorial.
